History of bodybuilding

From stage shows in London to gyms in every city, here is how the sport grew.

  • 1901

    Eugen Sandow runs the Great Competition at the Royal Albert Hall in London, one of the first public physique contests.

  • 1965

    Joe Gold opens Gold's Gym in Venice, California. The same year the first Mr. Olympia is held, won by Larry Scott.

  • 1977

    The film Pumping Iron brings Arnold Schwarzenegger and the sport to a world audience, and gyms fill up.

  • 1984 to 1991

    Lee Haney wins eight Mr. Olympia titles in a row, setting a new standard for size with symmetry.

  • 1998 to 2005

    Ronnie Coleman wins eight Mr. Olympia titles, known for heavy lifting and huge training volume.

  • Today

    Lifters use wearables, smart scales and data to plan training. That is where FitCoach AI fits in.

Nutrition basics

Training only works when food and rest back it up.

🍗

Protein

Eggs, chicken, fish, lentils and dal in every main meal.

🍚

Carbs

Rice, oats and fruit before training for energy.

💧

Water

Aim for at least 2.5 litres on training days.

😴

Sleep

7 to 8 hours so your muscles can recover.
